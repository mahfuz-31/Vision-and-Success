\subsection*{Belief in All\=ah}
\textit{Tawh\=id} is of three ---\textit{Ar-rububiyyah, Al-uluhiyyah} and \textit{Al-asma' was-sif\=at}. If any of these three is neglected his belief is invalid.

\subsubsection*{Tawh\=id Ar-rububiyyah}
We believe in the \textit{rububiyyah} of All\=ah. It means He is the \textit{Rabb} (Owner), Creator, Provider, Protector, Sustainer, He who has control over all things. The idolators of \textit{Makkah} believed in the \textit{rububiyyah} of All\=ah. All\=ah tells about them,
}
\begin{center}
    \begin{RLtext}
        قُل لِّمَنِ ٱلْأَرْضُ وَمَن فِيهَآ إِن كُنتُمْ تَعْلَمُونَ 
    \end{RLtext}
    \textbf{Say, (O Muhammad), "To whom belongs the earth and whoever is in it, if you should know?"} [23:84]
    \begin{RLtext}
        سَيَقُولُونَ لِلَّهِۚ قُلْ أَفَلَا تَذَكَّرُونَ 
    \end{RLtext}
    \textbf{They will say, "To Allāh." Say, "Then will you not remember?"} [23:85]
    \begin{RLtext}
        قُلْ مَن رَّبُّ ٱلسَّمَٰوَٰتِ ٱلسَّبْعِ وَرَبُّ ٱلْعَرْشِ ٱلْعَظِيمِ 
    \end{RLtext}
    \textbf{Say, "Who is Lord of the seven heavens and Lord of the Great Throne?"} [23:86]
    \begin{RLtext}
        سَيَقُولُونَ لِلَّهِۚ قُلْ أَفَلَا تَتَّقُونَ 
    \end{RLtext}
    \textbf{They will say, "[They belong] to Allāh." Say, "Then will you not fear Him?"} [23:87]
    \begin{RLtext}
        قُلْ مَنۢ بِيَدِهِۦ مَلَكُوتُ كُلِّ شَىْءٍ وَهُوَ يُجِيرُ وَلَا يُجَارُ عَلَيْهِ إِن كُنتُمْ تَعْلَمُونَ  
    \end{RLtext}
    \textbf{Say, "In whose hand is the realm of all things - and He protects while none can protect against Him - if you should know?"} [23:88]
    \begin{RLtext}
        سَيَقُولُونَ لِلَّهِۚ قُلْ فَأَنَّىٰ تُسْحَرُونَ 
    \end{RLtext}
    \textbf{They will say, "[All belongs] to Allāh." Say, "Then how are you deluded?"} [23:89]
\end{center}
\textvtt{
They believed in all these about All\=ah, yet they are the people of hell/disbelieve.

\subsubsection*{Tawhid Al-uluhiyyah}
We believe in the \textit{uluhiyyah} of All\=ah. It means He is the only true deity, who has the right our all types of worship. All other deities are false except Him. 

\subsubsection*{Tawhid Asma' Was-sif\=at}
We believe in all the names and attributes of All\=ah that are mentioned in the Qur\=an and \textit{Sunnah}.

\subsubsection*{Oneness of All\=ah}
We believe in the oneness of All\=ah. It means All\=ah has no partners in His \textit{rububiyyah, uluhiyyah and asma' was-sif\=at}. All\=ah says in the Qur\=an,
}
\begin{center}
    \begin{RLtext}
        رَّبُّ ٱلسَّمَٰوَٰتِ وَٱلْأَرْضِ وَمَا بَيْنَهُمَا فَٱعْبُدْهُ وَٱصْطَبِرْ لِعِبَٰدَتِهِۦۚ هَلْ تَعْلَمُ لَهُۥ سَمِيًّا 
    \end{RLtext}
    \textbf{Lord of the heavens and the earth and whatever is between them - so worship Him and have patience for His worship. Do you know of any similarity to Him?} [19:65]
\end{center}
\textvtt{

\subsubsection*{The Knowledge, Kingdom and Power of All\=ah}
We believe that---
}
\begin{center}
    \begin{RLtext}
        ٱللَّهُ لَآ إِلَٰهَ إِلَّا هُوَ ٱلْحَىُّ ٱلْقَيُّومُۚ لَا تَأْخُذُهُۥ سِنَةٌ وَلَا نَوْمٌۚ لَّهُۥ مَا فِى ٱلسَّمَٰوَٰتِ وَمَا فِى ٱلْأَرْضِۗ مَن ذَا ٱلَّذِى يَشْفَعُ عِندَهُۥٓ إِلَّا بِإِذْنِهِۦۚ يَعْلَمُ مَا بَيْنَ أَيْدِيهِمْ وَمَا خَلْفَهُمْۖ وَلَا يُحِيطُونَ بِشَىْءٍ مِّنْ عِلْمِهِۦٓ إِلَّا بِمَا شَآءَۚ وَسِعَ كُرْسِيُّهُ ٱلسَّمَٰوَٰتِ وَٱلْأَرْضَۖ وَلَا يَـُٔودُهُۥ حِفْظُهُمَاۚ وَهُوَ ٱلْعَلِىُّ ٱلْعَظِيمُ 
    \end{RLtext}
\end{center}
\textbf{
    Allāh - there is no deity except Him, the Ever-Living, the Self-Sustaining. Neither drowsiness overtakes Him nor sleep. To Him belongs whatever is in the heavens and whatever is on the earth. Who is it that can intercede with Him except by His permission? He knows what is [presently] before them and what will be after them, and they encompass not a thing of His knowledge except for what He wills. His Kursī extends over the heavens and the earth, and their preservation tires Him not. And He is the Most High, the Most Great.} [2:255]

\textvtt{
We believe that---
}
\begin{center}
    \begin{RLtext}
        هُوَ ٱللَّهُ ٱلَّذِى لَآ إِلَٰهَ إِلَّا هُوَۖ عَٰلِمُ ٱلْغَيْبِ وَٱلشَّهَٰدَةِۖ هُوَ    ٱلرَّحْمَٰنُ ٱلرَّحِيمُ \\
        هُوَ ٱللَّهُ ٱلَّذِى لَآ إِلَٰهَ إِلَّا هُوَ ٱلْمَلِكُ ٱلْقُدُّوسُ ٱلسَّلَٰمُ ٱلْمُؤْمِنُ    ٱلْمُهَيْمِنُ ٱلْعَزِيزُ ٱلْجَبَّارُ ٱلْمُتَكَبِّرُۚ سُبْحَٰنَ ٱللَّهِ عَمَّا يُشْرِكُونَ \\
        هُوَ ٱللَّهُ ٱلْخَٰلِقُ ٱلْبَارِئُ ٱلْمُصَوِّرُۖ لَهُ ٱلْأَسْمَآءُ ٱلْحُسْنَىٰۚ يُسَبِّحُ لَهُۥ مَا فِى ٱلسَّمَٰوَٰتِ وَٱلْأَرْضِۖ وَهُوَ ٱلْعَزِيزُ ٱلْحَكِيمُ 
    \end{RLtext}
\end{center}
\textbf{He is Allāh, other than whom there is no deity, Knower of the unseen and the witnessed. He is the Entirely Merciful, the Especially Merciful. He is Allāh, other than whom there is no deity, the Sovereign, the Pure, the Perfection, the Grantor of Security, the Overseer, the Exalted in Might, the Compeller, the Superior. Exalted is Allāh above whatever they associate with Him. He is Allāh, the Creator, the Producer, the Fashioner; to Him belong the best names. Whatever is in the heavens and earth is exalting Him. And He is the Exalted in Might, the Wise.} [59:22-24]

\textvtt{
We believe that---
}
\begin{center}

        أَوْ يُزَوِّجُهُمْ ذُكْرَانًا وَإِنَٰثًاۖ وَيَجْعَلُ مَن يَشَآءُ عَقِيمًاۚ إِنَّهُۥ عَلِيمٌ قَدِيرٌ

We believe that---

Indeed the word of All\=ah is not apart from Him, and none of His part is created.\footnote{Im\=am of ahlus sunnah, Abu Abdullah Ahmed Ibn Muhammad Ibn Hamble (may All\=ah have mercy upon him) said in his 'Us\=ul-us-Sunnah'---} All\=ah says,

\subsubsection*{All\=ah is with His Creation}
We believe that All\=ah is with His Creation. But it doesn't mean He is with them by Himself, indeed He is on His throne. From His throne, He knows all about His creation. He listens and watches everything. Gives kingdom to whom he wants and takes away kingdom from whom He wants. He is able and capable over all things from His throne. We don't say like the \textit{'Hululiyyah (Jahmiyyah)} that, All\=ah is with his creation on the same earth/place. 
}
\begin{center}
    \begin{RLtext}
        لَيْسَ كَمِثْلِهِۦ شَىْءٌۖ وَهُوَ ٱلسَّمِيعُ ٱلْبَصِيرُ \\

\subsubsection*{All\=ah's Will is of Two Types}
We believe that--- All\=ah's will is of two types: 1. \textit{Kauniyyah} 2. \textit{Shar'iyyah}
}
\begin{enumerate}
    \textvtt{
    \item \textit{Kauniyyah}: By this type of will of All\=ah, the task is completed. But is not necessary that All\=ah would like that. Like All\=ah says,}
    \begin{center}
        \begin{RLtext}
             وَلَوْ شَآءَ ٱللَّهُ مَا ٱقْتَتَلُوا۟ وَلَٰكِنَّ ٱللَّهَ يَفْعَلُ مَا يُرِيدُ 
        \end{RLtext}
        \textbf{And if Allāh had willed, they would not have fought each other, but Allāh does what He intends.} [2:253]
    \end{center}
    \textvtt{
    \item \textit{Shar'iyyah}: By this type of will, the task doesn't need to be fulfilled which All\=ah wills. But in this case, the matter has to be liked by All\=ah.}
    \begin{center}
        \begin{RLtext}
            وَٱللَّهُ يُرِيدُ أَن يَتُوبَ عَلَيْكُمْ    
        \end{RLtext}
        \textbf{All\=ah wants to accept your repentance.} [4:27]
    \end{center}
\end{enumerate}
\textvtt{
We believe that--- both wills of All\=ah are within His \textit{'Hikmah} (wisdom). In this matter, we may or may not understand some of His wisdom. It doesn't matter, whether our intellect realizes it or not. In all conditions, All\=ah is the greatest \textit{'H\=akim}.
}
\begin{center}
    \begin{RLtext}
        أَلَيْسَ ٱللَّهُ بِأَحْكَمِ ٱلْحَٰكِمِينَ 
    \end{RLtext}
    \textbf{Is not Allāh the most just of judges?} [95:8]
\end{center}

\textvtt{
We believe that it is inappropriate to interpret the Quran and Sunnah in a way that contradicts the names and attributes of All\=ah. Those who have changed these names and attributes of All\=ah, and used them in a purpose other than the purpose of All\=ah and His Messenger (peace be upon him), we consider ourselves free from them.

We also abandon their path who distorted, altered or twisted the names and attributes of All\=ah, and used them in a way that contradicts the purpose of All\=ah and His Messenger (peace be upon him).

Similarly, we abandon the path of those who make the names and attributes of All\=ah \textit{Ta'til} (meaningless) and leave the meaning purposed by All\=ah and His Messenger (peace be upon him). 

We also abandon the path of those who are called 'Mugalin,' meaning those who exceed limits and exaggerate in relation to the attributes of All\=ah, applying them in a specific form or creating their own interpretations to explain their meaning.

We firmly know that everything mentioned in the Qur'\=an and Sunnah is true and correct. Nothing contradicts or opposes each other.

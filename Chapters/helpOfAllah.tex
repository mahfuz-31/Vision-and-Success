\chapter{The Divine Help of All\=ah} 

\textvtt{
There are many reasons for which the divine help of All\=ah comes down to His servants. But here I'll mention the most important five reasons (according to my understanding) for getting All\=ah's divine help at the time of calamity. They are all from the \textit{Kit\=ab} and the \textit{Sunnah}.

\section*{The First Requirement: True Belief}
All\=ah \textit{rabbul 'izzah} says,
}
\begin{center}
    \begin{RLtext}
            وَكَانَ حَقًّا عَلَيْنَا نَصْرُ ٱلْمُؤْمِنِينَ 
    \end{RLtext}
    \textbf{And (it is a duty) incumbent upon Us was to support (help) the believers.} [30:47]
\end{center}
\textvtt{
Hence the first requirement to attain the help of All\=ah \textit{rabbul 'izzah} is to be (true) believers. Then what is believe (\textit{\=im\=an})? The Had\=ith of \textit{Jibril} peace be upon him we know the definition or the articles of belief (\textit{\=im\=an}),
}
\begin{RLtext}
    عَنْ عُمَرَ رَضِيَ اللهُ عَنْهُ أَيْضًا قَالَ:\\
    بَيْنَمَا نَحْنُ جُلُوسٌ عِنْدَ رَسُولِ اللَّهِ صلى الله عليه و سلم ذَاتَ يَوْمٍ، إذْ طَلَعَ عَلَيْنَا رَجُلٌ شَدِيدُ بَيَاضِ الثِّيَابِ، شَدِيدُ سَوَادِ الشَّعْرِ، لَا يُرَى عَلَيْهِ أَثَرُ السَّفَرِ، وَلَا يَعْرِفُهُ مِنَّا أَحَدٌ. حَتَّى جَلَسَ إلَى النَّبِيِّ صلى الله عليه و سلم . فَأَسْنَدَ رُكْبَتَيْهِ إلَى رُكْبَتَيْهِ، وَوَضَعَ كَفَّيْهِ عَلَى فَخِذَيْهِ،\\
    وَقَالَ: يَا مُحَمَّدُ أَخْبِرْنِي عَنْ الْإِسْلَامِ.\\
    . . .\\
    وَقَالَ: يَا مُحَمَّدُ أَخْبِرْنِي عَنْ الْإِسْلَامِ.\\
    قَالَ: أَنْ تُؤْمِنَ بِاَللَّهِ وَمَلَائِكَتِهِ وَكُتُبِهِ وَرُسُلِهِ وَالْيَوْمِ الْآخِرِ، وَتُؤْمِنَ بِالْقَدَرِ خَيْرِهِ وَشَرِّهِ.\\
    . . .\\
    [رَوَاهُ مُسْلِمٌ]
\end{RLtext}

\textbf{While we were one day sitting with the Messenger of Allah (peace be upon him), there appeared before us a man dressed in extremely white clothes and with very black hair. No traces of journeying were visible on him, and none of us knew him. He sat down close by the Prophet (peace be upon him), rested his knee against his thighs, and said, "O Muhammad! Inform me about Isl\=am."\\

but he went on to say, "Inform me about Im\=an."\\
He (the Messenger of Allah) answered, "It is that you believe in Allah and His angels and His Books and His Messengers and in the Last Day, and in Qadar (fate), both in its good and in its evil aspects."\\
. . .\\
}

\textvtt{
These are the articles of Im\=an. Every Muslim must know them by heart. But true belief—at a level that influences your actions—is what brings the help of All\=ah. For example, if I give you a bottle of poison and tell you to drink it, and you know that it is poison, you would say, "I believe it will kill me". That is the believe that affects your concious behavior. Similarly, to believe in All\=ah \textit{rabbul 'izzah} to the extent that it affects your actions, is the true belief, and that is the first requirement to attain the divine help of All\=ah \textit{rabbul 'izzah}. It is the belief where one knows All\=ah \textit{rabbul 'izzah} so well and so intimately that when he relies, he relies on All\=ah \textit{rabbul 'izzah} alone, and when he fears, he fears All\=ah \textit{rabbul 'izzah} alone, and when he hopes, he hopes in All\=ah \textit{rabbul 'izzah} alone.

One day, the Prophet \textit{salallahu 'alayhi wa sallam} was taking a nap in one of the journeys. He had hung his sword on a tree. One of the disbelievers came and took the sword \footnote{The Arabian swords on those days used to be so sharp that, people used to shave their baby's hair by them.}, pulled it out and put it one the blessed neck of the Prophet \textit{salallahu 'alayhi wa sallam} and said, "Who will save you from me today?" The Prophet \textit{salallahu 'alayhi wa sallam} replied, "All\=ah." The man's hands froze, and the sword fell from his hands. The Prophet \textit{salallahu 'alayhi wa sallam} took it and put it on his neck and said, "Who will save you from me today?"

He \textit{salallahu 'alayhi wa sallam} was in the cave of \textit{Thawr} at the time of \textit{Hijrah} with his beloved companion Abu Bakr \textit{radiyallahu 'anhu}. If I descrive the cave a little bit--- its a small little cave with two entrances, init there is a table like elevated rock, from the bottom hole if someone slightly bends down and peeks he would see if anyone is inside and from the upper hole if someone looks down he would see inside.The cave is at the peek of the mountain and the mountain is about 2500 ft high. It doesn't make any sense that the enemies climbed that rocky mountain and didn't look inside the cave. From inside the cave, they could see the feets of the enemies. The scholars of \textit{Seerah} say that, one of the enemies went up of the cave to answer the call of nature, if he looked down he would them. At that time Abu Bakr \textit{radiyallahu 'anhu} said to the Prophet \textit{salallahu 'alayhi wa sallam}, "If one of them looked down, they would see us." The Prophet \textit{salallahu 'alayhi wa sallam} said,
}

\begin{center}
    \begin{RLtext}
        لَا تَحْزَنْ إِنَّ ٱللَّهَ مَعَنَا 
    \end{RLtext}
    \textbf{Do not grieve; indeed All\=ah is with us [with His Knowledge].} [9:40]
\end{center}
\textvtt{
This shows the strength of the true belief. What am going to afraid of, when All\=ah \textit{rabbul 'izzah} is with me? \textit{Subh\=anall\=ah} All\=ah \textit{rabbul 'izzah} saved them from there.

So belief in All\=ah \textit{rabbul 'izzah} to the level that your heart becomes free from the fear of anything besides All\=ah. When you believe, you enter a seperate league. A league of the believers and Prophets, who don't look at means anymore. Because All\=ah does things through means, without means, and against means. Then every other person is thinking where am I going to get food from for my kids and you are thinking, 
}
\begin{center}
    \begin{RLtext}
 إِنَّ ٱللَّهَ هُوَ ٱلرَّزَّاقُ ذُو ٱلْقُوَّةِ ٱلْمَتِينُ
    \end{RLtext}
    \textbf{Indeed, Allah [alone] is the Supreme Provider—Lord of all Power, Ever Mighty.} [51:58]
\end{center}
\textvtt{
Like Yousuf \textit{alayhis salam}, they said, you'll go to prison. He said,
}
\begin{center}
    \begin{RLtext}
        رَبِّ ٱلسِّجْنُ أَحَبُّ إِلَيَّ مِمَّا يَدْعُونَنِيٓ إِلَيْهِ 
    \end{RLtext}
    \textbf{My Lord! Prison is dearer to me than that to which they invite me.} [12:33]
\end{center}
\textvtt{
Hunger--- All\=ah \textit{rabbul 'izzah} will feed me. Sickness--- All\=ah \textit{rabbul 'izzah} will cure me. Guidance--- All\=ah \textit{rabbul 'izzah} will guide me.
}
\begin{center}
    \begin{RLtext}
        قَالَ كَلَّآۖ إِنَّ مَعِيَ رَبِّي سَيَهۡدِينِ
    \end{RLtext}
    \textbf{[Musa (Moses)] said: "Nay, verily with me is my Lord. He will guide me."} [26:62]
\end{center}
\textvtt{

At the battle of \textit{Qadisiyyah}, when Sa'ad Ibn Abi Waqqas \textit{radiyallahu 'anhu} needed to cross the Tigris river with his army, and most of them including himself didn't know how to swim, he and his men crossed it with their horses saying,
}
\begin{center}
    \begin{RLtext}
        نستعينُهُ وَنَتَوَكَّلُ عَلَيْهِ، حسبُنَا ٱللَّهُ وَنِعْمَ ٱلْوَكِيلُ، ولاحولَ وَلا قُوَّةَ إِلَّا بِٱللَّهِ العلِيِّ العَظِيمِ
    \end{RLtext}
    We seek His help and rely upon Him. Sufficient for us is Allah, and [He is] the best Disposer of affairs. There is no power and no strength except with Allah, the Most High, the Most Great.
\end{center}
\textvtt{
When he took the plunge in the river, there was no doubt in his heart that--- I might sink or I might not sink.

So the first requirement to attain the help of All\=ah \textit{rabbul 'izzah} is to be true believers.

Hence, my beloved brother, strengthen your beliefs. \textit{Im\=an} increases with obedience and remeberance of All\=ah \textit{rabbul 'izzah}. The closer you're to All\=ah \textit{rabbul 'izzah}, the more \textit{Ima\=an} will fill your heart.  
}

\section*{The Second Requirement: Be on the Truth / be helper of All\=ah}

\textvtt{
All\=ah \textit{rabbul 'izzah} says,
}
\begin{center}
    \begin{RLtext}
    إِن تَنصُرُواْ ٱللَّهَ يَنصُرۡكُمۡ
    \end{RLtext}
    \textbf{So long as you're helping All\=ah, He will help you.} [47:7]
\end{center}
\textvtt{
Meaning, so long as you are on the truth and you're going to the cause of what is the truth and the cause of the \textit{deen}, All\=ah \textit{rabbul 'izzah} will help you.
}

\section*{The Third Requirement: Unity}

\textvtt{
One of the generals of one of the cities of Persia was fighting to the end with the Muslims. But when the Muslims took over the city he locked himself up to a tower. The Muslims came around him and was trying to get him out. He said, "Listen Muslims! I have a hundred arrows in my quiver, and each one will kill one of you. Does one man worth a hundred of you?" They said, "What do you want?" He said, "I'll surrender if you place my hand on the hand of your \textit{khalifa 'Umar Ibn Al-Khattab} \textit{radiyallahu 'anhu}." They said, "Okay." They took him to Madina. When they reached Madina, they went to the house of \textit{'Umar} and knocked and said, "Where is \textit{amirul mu'mineen}". "He is not here, he has gone to see a delegation from Kufa. He is in the masjid." So they went to the masjid and he was not in the masjid. This man, the general, was being parraded in chains through the madina with his lavish clothings. It was a sight in Madina. Because those days persian kings had rubies and emeralds in their clothings, and they were very luxurious. And in Madina this was not the norm, they were very simple people. Some kids came--- Are you looking for the \textit{amirul mu'mineen}? They said, "Yes." They said, "He is sleeping there besides the masjid." So they went and found \textit{'Umar Ibn Al-Khattab} \textit{radiyallahu 'anhu} sleeping under a tree putting his outer garment under his head. They didn't want to disturb the \textit{khalifa}, so they stood there. The general asked, "Where is \textit{'Umar}?" They said, (Subhanallah) "He is sleeping there." He said, "You're the people who've just conquered Persia. Where is his guards! Where is the court! Where is his palace! Where is the scribes! Where are the people who fan him!" They said, "No guards, no scribes, no nothing. This is the \textit{khalifa} of the believers." In talking \textit{'Umar} \textit{radiyallahu 'anhu} woke up. When he woke up, said,"Who is this enemy of himself? [As he saw such lavish cloths on him] Is this the general so and so?" They said, "Yes." He said, "Go and put simple clothing on him. I won't talk to him when he is dressed like this." So they put simple clothing on him and he sat, looking nurvous. \textit{'Umar radiyallahu 'anhu} said, "Tell me, what is your situation? don't you see that how All\=ah has disgraced you after all the pillagig, robbery, unjustice to your citizens and you broke your oath with me time and time again?" Then this man says, "Before [Islam] we used to fight you. It was us against you. They was no All\=ah on your side or on our side [i.e. All\=ah \textit{rabbul 'izzah} wouldn't help you and wouldn't help us]. We fed better against you and we bit you. But now, you became believers, and All\=ah \textit{rabbul 'izzah} is on your side and helps you. There is no All\=ah on our side so we loose and All\=ah has disgraced us." [He meant that, it's not your men that is bringing victory after victory. It is All\=ah on your side.] Then \textit{'Umar radiyallahu 'anhu} said, "And our unit." 

The unity itself is a help of All\=ah \textit{rabbul 'izzah}. All\=ah \textit{rabbul 'izzah} says,
}
\begin{center}
    \begin{RLtext}
        وَأَلَّفَ بَيۡنَ قُلُوبِهِمۡۚ لَوۡ أَنفَقۡتَ مَا فِي ٱلۡأَرۡضِ جَمِيعٗا مَّآ أَلَّفۡتَ بَيۡنَ قُلُوبِهِمۡ وَلَٰكِنَّ ٱللَّهَ أَلَّفَ بَيۡنَهُمۡۚ إِنَّهُۥ عَزِيزٌ حَكِيمٞ

    \end{RLtext}
\end{center}
\textbf{And He has united their (i.e. believers’) hearts. If you had spent all that is in the earth, you could not have united their hearts, but Allah has united them. Certainly He is All-Mighty, All-Wise.} [8:63]
